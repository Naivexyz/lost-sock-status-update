One-line entry for the **Funniest Lost Sock Status Update** competition on
[make.fun](https://make.fun/competitions/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-lost-sock-status-update-4b1fa3ce).

## The line

> Update: the dryer didn't eat me. I left. I live in the garden now, and the gnome respects me more than you ever did.

116 characters, in the voice of a lost sock that walked out — the dryer didn't
eat it, the garden gnome respects it more than its owner ever did. The
punchline lands in the last few words, and the obvious sock material (the
dryer, the missing partner) gets twisted somewhere unexpected.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that single status update to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8,
1–120 characters, mode `0644`):

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
